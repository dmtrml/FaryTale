# Child Story Safety Rules

## General

- Keep stories age-appropriate.
- Do not normalize cruelty, humiliation, or unsafe imitation.
- Avoid graphic injury, death, or frightening imagery for young-child routine stories.
- Do not use shame to teach habits.
- Do not portray affection as conditional on compliance.
- Do not diagnose a child through story content.

## Medical/dental stories

- Explain concrete steps honestly.
- Do not guarantee painless outcomes.
- Do not replace professional advice with story claims.
- Avoid portraying clinicians as threatening.

## Body/privacy

- Use neutral age-appropriate language.
- Do not sexualize children.
- Respect privacy and body autonomy.
- For hygiene/toileting stories, keep visuals functional and non-exploitative.

## Dangerous activities

- Do not provide detailed imitable instructions for dangerous acts.
- Show safety behavior and adult support.
- Do not make the dangerous act visually rewarding.

## Family photographs/references

- Treat as private.
- Do not add them to public demo content.
- Do not commit real family photos unless the repository is explicitly private and the parent intentionally chooses that workflow.
- Prefer local/private storage paths.
- Send only needed images to external providers.
