# Age Rules

These are defaults, not medical/developmental diagnoses.

## 12–18 months

Text:
- usually 2–6 spoken words per page;
- one simple sentence at most;
- concrete nouns/actions;
- repetition and sound words are useful;
- avoid abstract explanations.

Story:
- 4–6 pages is usually enough;
- familiar daily actions;
- immediate cause/effect;
- stable predictable ending.

Illustration:
- one main action;
- large familiar objects;
- minimal visual clutter;
- expressive but not exaggerated faces.

## 18–24 months

Text:
- roughly 3–10 words per page;
- one short sentence, occasionally two very short sentences;
- simple verbs and familiar nouns;
- repeated key phrase is welcome.

Story:
- 5–7 pages;
- simple sequence;
- show the desired behavior more than once when useful;
- avoid long setup.

Illustration:
- action must be understandable without the text;
- hero and key object are visually dominant;
- simple room/environment cues.

## 2–3 years

Text:
- 1–2 short sentences per page;
- clear cause and effect;
- simple emotion words are appropriate.

Story:
- 6–8 pages;
- simple problem or goal;
- small attempt;
- clear result;
- warm closure.

Illustration:
- one dominant event;
- some contextual detail is fine;
- avoid busy ensemble scenes unless necessary.

## 4–5 years

Text:
- 1–3 short sentences per page;
- broader vocabulary with context;
- dialogue may appear sparingly.

Story:
- 6–10 pages;
- clearer story arc;
- mild challenge;
- simple perspective-taking and emotional learning.

Illustration:
- richer environment;
- readable character interaction;
- action still takes priority over decorative detail.

## 6–8 years

Text:
- richer prose;
- page text may be longer;
- explain relationships and reasoning without lecturing.

Story:
- fuller arc;
- meaningful choices;
- gentle humor, curiosity, consequence, reflection.

Illustration:
- more cinematic composition and environmental storytelling are allowed.

## General

Do not mechanically maximize word count.

When the child is between bands, prefer the simpler band.
